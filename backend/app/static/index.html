Scientific Methodology

Answers to the 5 core scientific questions using empirical NASA Earth observation data.

The 5 Core Scientific Questions

1. WHAT is changing?

Near-surface air temperature (T2M), precipitation (PRECTOTCORR), and surface solar downward radiation (SW_DWN).

2. WHERE is it changing?

Dense 30-station grid covering Cairo, Alexandria, Nile Delta, Suez Canal, and Middle Egypt.

3. HOW MUCH is it changing?

Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) regression slope and robust Theil-Sen median slopes reported per year and per decade.

4. HOW does it change over time?

Decomposes 26 years of monthly records (2000–2025), removing 12-month seasonality to evaluate multi-decadal trends.

5. IS it statistically significant?

Two-sided p-values and Mann-Kendall monotonic rank tests evaluated at α = 0.05 with exact 95% confidence intervals.
